Research philosophy

Nature is quantum.
How do we simulate it?

My work connects quantum information science with the mechanics of real, multiscale systems. The common thread is a desire to model what is difficult: interacting particles, turbulent flow, cloud microphysics, and nonlinear evolution.

At Pacific Northwest National Laboratory, I lead research on efficient end-to-end quantum algorithms for plasma physics and contribute to atmospheric and computational science.

Research areas

One toolkit, many scales

From subcellular dynamics to clouds and interstellar matter, I study how computation can reveal nonlinear systems.
